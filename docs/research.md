# Selection evidence: Mixpanel

Checked 24 October 2026. New registry family: product-analytics. This is separate from subscription revenue analytics and customer survey operations.

The vendor publishes USD 1,635/month with annual billing for twenty million events each month: USD 19,620/year. The monthly-billed option is USD 2,289/month. This establishes a five-figure price scenario, not a verified invoice for a particular business. Smaller plans are cheaper, and an eligible free plan exists. The comparison does not assume every Mixpanel customer pays this much.

Scores, each out of five: annual bill 3, data-shaped 4, buyer fit 2, search demand 3, one-session buildable 4, import path 5. Total 21/30. Buyer fit is conservative: permitted local customer and CRM snapshots contained no confirmed Mixpanel use, and no live CRM was accessed. Vendor customer material shows product teams use it, but is not evidence of an Enterprise DNA customer's invoice. Search demand is qualitative, not measured keyword volume.

Other candidates: D4H 3/2/2/2/2/4 = 15/30, rejected because its real-time response coordination requires a frontend and cannot honestly be replaced by this lane's database-only build. Crayon 1/4/2/3/4/3 = 17/30, rejected because the public evidence did not establish a five-figure annual price or the exact export fields. Both families were absent from the build registry. Mixpanel had the strongest verified price and import evidence of this shortlist.

Sources opened this run:

- [Mixpanel pricing](https://mixpanel.com/pricing/): event-volume prices, annual and monthly billing, free allowance, paid features. Figures above are from the vendor's explicit pricing breakdown, not an aggregator.
- [Mixpanel raw event export](https://docs.mixpanel.com/reference/raw-event-export): JSONL events, export date range and timezone qualification.
- [Mixpanel event model](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/data-structure/events-and-properties): names, timestamps, identities and properties.
- [Mixpanel customer index](https://mixpanel.com/customers/all/): product-analytics usage examples. No third-party customer names are used in our page.
- [D4H pricing](https://www.d4h.com/pricing/united-states) and [export help](https://help.d4h.com/article/566-exporting-to-a-spreadsheet): published response product prices and spreadsheet export.
- [Crayon pricing](https://www.crayon.co/pricing-inquiry): tailored quotes, no public dollar figure verified.

The free base covers imported event analysis, measurement contracts, review evidence and paperwork. It does not provide production collection, Mixpanel's identity resolution, replay, experiments or feature serving. No incumbent inability, speed advantage or savings is claimed without evidence. The ten questions are concrete examples answered by the tested demo, not unsupported statements that Mixpanel cannot answer them.
